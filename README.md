# Loan-Risk-Customer-Default-Analysis-for-CrediTrust

## Overview

This project analyzes the loan portfolio performance of Creditrust by evaluating customer behavior, default trends, credit quality, employment status, income levels, and loan risk exposure. The analysis identifies key drivers of loan defaults and provides actionable recommendations to improve portfolio quality and reduce financial risk.

## Executive Summary

The analysis revealed a high portfolio default rate of 46.47%, indicating significant credit risk exposure across multiple customer segments. High-risk borrowers, unemployed customers, and low-income earners contributed disproportionately to defaults.

Key findings include:

* Total loan exposure reached $218M
* High-risk exposure accounted for 7.93% of the portfolio
* Nearly half of all customers defaulted
* Car and business loans recorded the highest default rates
* Credit score, employment status, and income level strongly influenced repayment behavior

  ## Major Findings

Customer Risk Analysis

* Unemployed borrowers showed the highest default tendency
* Low-income customers contributed heavily to high-risk classifications
* Previous defaulters remained high-risk borrowers
* Medium-risk customers contributed significantly to overall defaults

 Loan Product Analysis
 
* Car Loans recorded the highest default rate
* Business Loans also showed elevated repayment risk
* Personal loans had lower overall defaults but severe defaults among high-risk customers

 Credit Score Analysis
 
* Borrowers with very poor credit scores had the highest default rates
* Default risk declined as credit quality improved
* Even excellent credit-score customers showed notable repayment challenges

## Business Insights

* Employment stability is a major indicator of repayment performance
* Credit scoring alone is insufficient for loan approval decisions
* High-value loan concentration increases portfolio vulnerability
* Risk-tier classification is effective but requires stronger monitoring for medium-risk borrowers

## Recommendations

* Strengthen underwriting policies for high-risk applicants
* Improve affordability and credit assessments
* Introduce stricter monitoring for medium- and high-risk borrowers
* Reassess car and business loan approval standards
* Reduce exposure to repeat defaulters
* Enhance portfolio diversification strategies
* Implement advanced predictive risk models

## Conclusion

The Creditrust loan portfolio currently faces elevated credit risk due to high default levels among vulnerable borrower segments. Strengthening risk management, underwriting standards, and borrower monitoring processes will help improve repayment performance, reduce exposure, and enhance long-term portfolio stability.
